# Cover Letter Question Types

Classify every Korean cover letter question before drafting. Use the question type to choose the answer frame, not to force keywords into the answer.

| Type | Common Question Signal | Evaluation Intent | Answer Frame | Resume Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 경험형 | 경험, 사례, 도전, 실패, 성취 | concrete behavior and repeatable action | STAR or CAR: situation, task/problem, action, result | Experience Bank, projects, work history, metrics |
| 역량형 | 강점, 역량, 장점, 차별점 | capability with proof | strength, evidence, role application | repeated behavior, feedback, result |
| 지원동기형 | 지원동기, 왜 우리 회사, 관심 계기 | company/role understanding and fit | company/role understanding, personal connection, contribution plan | Target Roles, Career Vision, related experience |
| 직무형 | 직무, 역할, 수행, 기여 | job capability and role understanding | role understanding, evidence mapping, growth/contribution plan | role-relevant work, projects, tools, metrics |
| 협업/소통 | 협업, 갈등, 설득, 조율 | working with others under constraints | shared goal, conflict/context, action, result, learning | collaboration, conflict, stakeholder work |
| 문제해결 | 문제, 개선, 해결, 원인 | problem definition and execution quality | problem, diagnosis, action, result, prevention | Problem, Action, Result, prevention |
| 가치관/윤리 | 가치관, 원칙, 책임, 윤리 | judgment and trust | principle, difficult choice, action, consequence | principle-based decisions, responsibility examples |
| 입사후포부 | 입사 후, 계획, 성장, 비전 | realistic future contribution | near-term contribution, growth path, evidence basis | Career Vision, 1-Year Contribution, 3-Year Growth Direction |

## Length Handling

- Count Korean characters with spaces unless the company or question specifies another rule.
- If a limit exists, target 90-98% of the limit.
- If the draft exceeds the limit, compress the lowest-impact sentence first.
- If the draft is too short, add only resume-backed specifics.

## Natural Korean Writing

- Prefer concrete verbs over broad attitudes.
- Avoid generic claims such as "열정을 가지고", "빠르게 성장하겠습니다", and "최선을 다하겠습니다" unless paired with evidence.
- Keep each sentence interview-defensible from `resume.md`.
