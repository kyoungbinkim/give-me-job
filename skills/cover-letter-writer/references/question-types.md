# Cover Letter Question Types

Classify every Korean cover letter question before drafting. Use the question
type to choose the answer frame, not to force keywords into the answer.

| Type | Common Question Signal | Evaluation Intent | Answer Frame | Resume Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Experience | experience, example, challenge, failure, achievement | concrete behavior and repeatable action | STAR or CAR: situation, task/problem, action, result | Work Experience or Projects entries, strength comments, metrics |
| Strength | strength, capability, advantage, differentiation | capability with proof | strength, evidence, role application | repeated behavior, feedback, result |
| Motivation | why this company, why this role, interest trigger | company/role understanding and fit | company/role understanding, personal connection, contribution plan | Target Role, Summary, related Work Experience or Projects |
| Role Fit | role, duty, contribution, execution | job capability and role understanding | role understanding, evidence mapping, growth or contribution plan | role-relevant work, projects, tools, metrics |
| Collaboration | collaboration, conflict, persuasion, coordination | working with others under constraints | shared goal, conflict/context, action, result, learning | collaboration, conflict, stakeholder work |
| Problem Solving | problem, improvement, solution, cause | problem definition and execution quality | problem, diagnosis, action, result, prevention | problem, action, result, prevention |
| Values | values, principle, responsibility, ethics | judgment and trust | principle, difficult choice, action, consequence | principle-based decisions, responsibility examples |
| Future Plan | after joining, plan, growth, vision | realistic future contribution | near-term contribution, growth path, evidence basis | Target Role, Summary, strongest role-relevant evidence |

## Length Handling

- Count Korean characters with spaces unless the company or question specifies
  another rule.
- If a limit exists, target 90-98% of the limit.
- If the draft exceeds the limit, compress the lowest-impact sentence first.
- If the draft is too short, add only resume-backed specifics.

## Natural Korean Writing

- Prefer concrete verbs over broad attitudes.
- Avoid generic passion or growth claims unless paired with resume-backed
  behavior, method, or result.
- Keep each sentence interview-defensible from `resume.md`.
