# Cover Letter Rules

## Writing Principles

- Start from what the employer needs, then connect the candidate's evidence.
- Use concrete problem, action, and result.
- Prefer one strong experience over several shallow examples.
- Keep the company fit natural. Do not insert values keywords mechanically.
- Use metrics when present. If metrics are absent, ask about scale, frequency, period, users, throughput, or comparison before inventing numbers.
- Make each paragraph defensible in an interview.

## Safety Checks

- Does every strong claim map to a `resume.md` evidence ID?
- Is there any company name from another application?
- Is the candidate claiming a skill that appears nowhere in `resume.md`?
- Is the answer responding to the actual question?
- Is the sentence readable for an HR reviewer?
- Is the conclusion about company contribution specific enough?

## Source Guidance

The writing strategy follows these summarized sources:

- STAFFS STORY Naver blog, 2024-07-30: emphasize company research, JD keywords, STAR structure, measurable results, differentiation, logical flow, and matching company needs to applicant experience.
- Work24 cover letter guide: treat the cover letter as interview source material that shows role fit, company fit, motivation, and future contribution.

Do not quote either source in generated cover letters.
